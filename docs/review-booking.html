Cancellation & booking policy

Prototype policy only. Final operational, cancellation and refund rules will be approved by Sports Board Punjab before launch.

1. Booking confirmation

A booking is confirmed only after successful payment and issuance of a booking ID or digital pass.

2. Player responsibility

The booking holder is responsible for the conduct of all players included in the booking and for ensuring that venue rules are followed.

3. Arrival and court access

  • Players should arrive at least 15 minutes before the booked time.
  • Late arrival does not automatically extend the booked session.
  • The digital booking pass may be required at reception or court entry.

4. Cancellation

  • Cancellation is intended to be allowed without charge up to 6 hours before the session.
  • Late cancellation may result in partial or no refund.
  • No-show bookings may be treated as used bookings.

5. Refunds

Approved refunds will be returned through the original payment channel or SBP Padel wallet, depending on the final payment configuration.

6. Rescheduling

Rescheduling will be subject to court availability and the final SBP rescheduling window. Any price difference may be payable.

7. Venue interruption

If a court becomes unavailable because of weather, maintenance, official events or another venue-side reason, SBP may offer a replacement slot, wallet credit or refund according to the final approved policy.

8. Court use

  • Bookings are valid only for the selected court, date and time.
  • Players must use appropriate sports footwear and equipment.
  • Smoking, damaging court equipment, abusive conduct and unsafe play are prohibited.

9. Official events and closures

Sports Board Punjab may block courts for tournaments, maintenance or official use. Existing affected bookings will be handled under the final approved refund/rescheduling rules.
